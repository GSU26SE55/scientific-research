\section{Derivation of Equation~\eqref{eq:contrastive}}
\label{app:derivation}

Starting from the InfoNCE objective, we derive the bound used
in~\Cref{subsec:component2}. Let \(p(x,x^{+})\) denote the joint
distribution of positive pairs. Then
\begin{align}
  I(X; X^{+})
    &\geq \mathbb{E}\!\left[\log
       \frac{\exp(s(z, z^{+}))}{\frac{1}{N}\sum_j \exp(s(z, z_j))}\right]
       \nonumber \\
    &= -\mathcal{L}_{\text{con}} + \log N.
\end{align}
Maximizing the mutual information lower bound is therefore equivalent to
minimizing~\eqref{eq:contrastive}.
